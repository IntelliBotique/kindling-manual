§1.3: the protocol defines no chargeable surface at the connection layer. Every organization here except Mycelial is invented by the Kindling sites, to show what could be built.

Quotations are from the Kindling sites, quoted in K.3 and checked against each source page on October 4, 2026. The rows are in K.3’s order and are not sorted by size, popularity or revenue. Read K.3 for the three questions to ask of any price.

This edition of The Solo Operator’s Field Manual is for the people building on Kindling and the people keeping Pools on it. Kindling is a real open protocol, published by TranquilTech. Nothing runs on it at TranquilTech yet. Every example here carries a label: invented by the Kindling sites, or invented for this edition.
