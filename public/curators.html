The curator’s charter · in the Charter’s dress

For curators and the organizations that keep Pools.

Planned · shown with invented examples

What it’s for
Keeping a Pool: writing its charter and choosing its consent model (§3.2), holding a consent proof for every entry (§3.4), and paying for the work with something sold beside the introduction.
What it’s not for
Adding anyone who has not said yes, which §5.1 forbids. Charging two people to meet, which §1.3 names as a non-goal.

How will people come in?

Pick the consent model your manifest will name (§3.2, §5.4). Nothing is stored, and coming back to this page starts from nothing chosen.
