October 4, 2026. Cover the logo. The test is whether a reader can tell this edition from the other five by its first screen, its order, its first ask, its tools and examples, its register, its dress and its access model. The others are as Josh's eight-point page sets them on October 3, 2026.

**Where it could be mistaken.** The Library alone also opens without an email for some of its pages, but it is the whole book and opens on the plates. Attuned and Kindling share bones (token names, the 6px step, the motion budget and the Atkinson toggle are Attuned's, per the profile). Kindling never names neurodivergence, carries no member gate, and its first screen is a charter with two doors where Attuned's has two buttons and no doors. The Campaign edition is the only other edition with supplement chapters; its supplements are election law for California, and its first ask is a sentence the reader writes.

**Verdict.** With the logo covered, three things give the edition away on the first screen: the § on every claim, the two dresses side by side, and the "Nothing yet" chip. It is also the only edition that carries no Field Manual text.
